/Process

From concept to delivery

Step 01

Moodboard & Concept

I start by researching the brand's audience, gathering visual references, and locking a balanced 60-30-10 color palette and font pair before creating layouts.

60% 30% 10%
Step 02

Design & Refine

I build cohesive social feed grids, vertical story sequences, print stationery, and web mockups on consistent spacing grids, refining details until every board reads clearly.

Step 03

Final Delivery

I organize the finished boards into clean folders—exporting crisp RGB assets for digital feeds and web screens alongside CMYK files for print stationery.
